What it checks

Every policy, against the same checklist

The app reads the schedule, certificate, endorsement and debit note together, so it catches what one page alone won't show.

Must be present

Waive Betterment

Without it, the customer pays part of the cost of new parts on an older car.

Must be absent or RM 0

Voluntary Excess

Flags any voluntary excess the customer agreed to for a lower premium.

Must be present

Windscreen

Shows the windscreen limit written on the schedule.

Must be present

Special Perils

Flood, storm and landslide damage, which basic comprehensive cover leaves out.

Must be present

SRCC

Strike, riot and civil commotion damage.

Must be present

LLP and LLAN

Legal liability to passengers, and of passengers for negligent acts.

Must agree on every page

Premium and tax

Cover dates

Effective and expiry dates compared across all documents, with an expiry warning.

Why betterment matters

On a 7-year-old car, the customer can owe a quarter of the parts bill

Unless betterment is waived, the insurer deducts part of the cost of new parts on vehicles 5 years and older. The app works out the car's age from the policy and estimates the share before you quote.

Under 5 yrs0%
5 years15%
6 years20%
7 years25%
8 years30%
9 years35%
10+ years40%

Standard Malaysian scale; the insurer's adjuster has the final say.

How it works

Set it up once, then just read the results

Connect your Policy Inbox

Pick the folder where agents' policy PDFs are saved. The app checks it when opened and every 30 minutes after that. You can also drop PDFs straight onto the page.

It reads every new policy

Each PDF is checked once against the full list. Pages it can't read, such as scans, are flagged for a look by eye instead of guessed at.

Read the claims briefing

Claim essentials sit at the top of each policy, red for problems. A briefing is written automatically and saved in a Briefings folder, ready to print or share.

Privacy

Customer documents never leave your computer

  • PDFs are read inside your own browser. Nothing is uploaded to this website or anywhere else.
  • Results and the run log are kept in your browser and in your own Policy Inbox folder.
  • The app never contacts the insurer, the agent or the customer. You decide what to share.
  • An internet connection is only used to load the PDF reader.
